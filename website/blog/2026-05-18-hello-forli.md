CasaStudente is opening listings for the 2026/27 academic year.

We’re starting in Forlì because that’s where the student housing crisis is most acute and where a small team can actually change things on the ground: 2,426 new students enroll each year, 78% of them need housing, and a single new 45-bed studentato gets fully booked in 20 days. Meanwhile thousands of apartments sit empty.

{/* truncate */}

This release ships everything a student rental needs end-to-end: verified listings, university-verified accounts, Stripe-secured rent, AI-powered search and translation, roommate matching, Italian-law digital leases, and an admin console for moderation and disputes. It’s all open source — read the [introduction](/docs/intro), follow [getting started](/docs/getting-started), and tell us what we got wrong.

If you’re a Forlì student, a landlord, or just curious — there are a hundred ways to help. Start [here](/docs/contributing).
